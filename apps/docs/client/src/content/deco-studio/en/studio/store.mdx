## What is the Store?

The **Store** is where you pick which **registries** populate your [Connections](/en/studio/connections) catalog. A registry is a source of MCP servers; the catalog you see on the Connections page is the union of every registry you have enabled.

You'll find it under **Settings → Store**.

<Callout type="info">
  The Store is not where you install a connection — that happens on the **Connections** page. The Store just controls which MCPs show up there.
</Callout>

## Registry types

### Deco Store

The official, curated registry. Always available. Verified integrations tested for compatibility with deco Studio. This is the source for most common integrations.

### Community

A broader, community-sourced catalog (the MCP Registry). Useful when the Deco Store doesn't have what you need. Quality and maintenance vary.

### Private registries

Your organization can connect its own private MCP registry — useful for proprietary tools or internal services that shouldn't be shared externally. Click **Add registry** to connect one.

## Toggling registries

Each registry has a switch. Turn one off and its MCPs stop appearing in your Connections catalog. Existing connections you've already installed from that registry keep working — only the catalog is affected.
