## Studio is the platform. Solutions are what we build on it.

Studio is a generic control plane — agents, connections, automations, monitoring. On top of it, deco builds and offers **pre-packaged agents** for specific kinds of work.

This section documents those.

## Who they're for

Teams managing **online storefronts** and content-heavy **digital experiences** — ecommerce, marketplaces, media sites, branded direct-to-consumer.

## Available now

- **[Agentic CMS](/en/studio/solutions/agentic-cms)** — the evolution of [deco.cx](https://docs.deco.cx). A visual CMS for storefronts with AI agents that operate the site alongside your team, Git-native by design.

More storefront agents are in progressive rollout — see [decocms.com](https://www.decocms.com) for what's on the way.

<Callout type="info">
  All of these are themselves agents running on Studio. You can see them in your sidebar, attach the connections they need, monitor their tool calls, and tweak their instructions like any other agent. They're not a separate product.
</Callout>
