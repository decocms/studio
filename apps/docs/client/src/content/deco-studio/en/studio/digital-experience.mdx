## Studio is the platform. Digital Experience is what deco ships on top.

Studio is a generic control plane — agents, connections, automations, monitoring, all open-source. On top of it, deco ships a **Digital Experience** suite: packaged teams of agents tuned for building and running storefronts and content-driven sites, so you don't start from a blank org.

Today the most developed capabilities are for **enterprise commerce**.

## Storefront Autopilot

AI agents that **monitor, fix, and improve your storefront 24/7** — while your team stays in control. Built on Studio plus the deco.cx CMS, it pairs a visual-and-code editor with a roster of specialist agents that handle the recurring operational work:

- **Implementation & design** — port a storefront to deco's stack, and generate components that match your design system, tokens, and conventions
- **Performance & reliability** — continuous Lighthouse on every PR, plus error-spike detection that opens the fix
- **SEO, catalog & content** — technical SEO, PLP reordering, catalog enrichment, and blog/content authoring

Every agent works the way the rest of Studio does: visible in your sidebar, opening PRs you review, with every action logged and costed. Humans and agents share the same editor — nothing happens behind your back.

→ See the full roster and results at **[decocms.com](https://www.decocms.com)**.

## The flagship: Agentic CMS

The **[Agentic CMS](./agentic-cms)** is the heart of the Digital Experience suite — the evolution of [deco.cx](https://docs.deco.cx). A visual CMS for storefronts where AI agents author content, adjust the design, enrich the catalog, and keep the site healthy alongside your team — Git-native by design.

<Callout type="info">
  These capabilities aren't a separate product — each one is a configuration of agents running on Studio. Everything you learn about Studio [agents](./agents), [connections](./connections), and [automations](./automations) applies directly.
</Callout>
