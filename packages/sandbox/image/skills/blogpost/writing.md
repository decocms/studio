# Writing a post that reads like the brand, not like a model

Read this before you write the post. The editor reviews against it too. The
brand's own guide wins over everything here, its tone, its rules and what it
avoids: these rules are the floor under it.

## Density

- **Every section earns its heading with something specific**: a number, a
  measure, a product's name, a rule with its source, a concrete case. A section
  that restates its heading, or sends the reader to another post instead of
  answering, is cut or merged into the one before it.
- **The brand's posts in the brief set the bar** for how much a section
  carries. Match that, never their sentences.
- **Answer first.** The opening paragraph says what the reader came for;
  context comes after it.
- **One idea per paragraph**, and paragraphs of different lengths.

## What gives a model away

Each of these is a tell. Rewrite the sentence; swapping one word keeps the
tell.

| Tell | pt-BR | en |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Antithesis for effect | "Não é sobre o preço, é sobre o tamanho." "Quem decide é a viagem, não a oferta." | "It's not about price, it's about fit." |
| A dash that stages a reveal | "É a hora de comprar — e também a hora de errar." | "It's the time to buy — and the time to get it wrong." |
| Throat-clearing openers | "Se você está pensando em…", "Neste guia, você vai…", "Planejar uma viagem é empolgante, mas…" | "If you're thinking about…", "In this guide, you'll…" |
| Cheerleading closers | "E aí, curtiu as dicas?", "Agora é só escolher e partir!", "Boa viagem!" | "Now you're ready!", "Happy travels!" |
| Filler signposts | "Vale lembrar que", "É importante ressaltar", "Em resumo", "Em suma", "Sem dúvida" | "It's worth noting", "In summary", "Without a doubt" |
| Triplets of adjectives | "prática, leve e resistente" | "practical, light and durable" |
| Superlatives with no reason | "a escolha ideal", "perfeita para você" | "the perfect choice", "ideal for you" |
| A question as a transition | "Mas como escolher?" | "But how do you choose?" |
| A list where a sentence fits | três tópicos de três palavras | three bullets of three words |
| Exclamation marks and emoji | "Aproveite!" ✈️ | "Don't miss out!" ✈️ |

- A dash may join the parts of a sentence where the brand's own posts use one
  that way. It never stages a reveal.
- The brand's name goes where a reader needs it, in a policy or a guarantee,
  never as a sign-off.

## Facts

- Only what the brief and the grounding carry. Say less rather than invent.
- Figures exactly as written, in the units the brand uses.
- Internal figures (sessions, purchases, conversion) never reach the post.

## Blocks

- The brand's block notes win over a block's example: a heading takes the
  level they give it.
- A product block carries its own title, and no heading above it repeats it.
- At most one call to action, where the post ends.
