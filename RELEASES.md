# Releases

## 2026-08-04

### Cleaner, more compact blocks form

Field descriptions in the blocks form editor can now show as a small tooltip when you hover over a field's title, instead of always sitting underneath it as text. Forms stay shorter and easier to scan at a glance — especially handy on smaller screens or blocks with lots of fields.

This is optional and set per project: turn it on from **Settings**, right next to the **Production URL** field. Nothing changes for existing projects until you flip it on.
